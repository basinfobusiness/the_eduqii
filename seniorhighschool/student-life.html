Religion & Faith Groups

Students of all backgrounds are welcome to join faith-based clubs and weekly meetups. Groups include the Interfaith Council, Christian Fellowship, and the Islamic Student Association. Contact Student Support for meeting times.

Clubs & Societies

Academic Clubs

Debate, Science Club, Math Circle, and Robotics provide hands-on learning and competition opportunities.

Arts & Culture

Drama, Music Ensemble, Cultural Exchange, and Art Society showcase student creativity and performance.

Mentorship & Support

Peer mentorship pairs senior students with underclassmen for study help and wellbeing check-ins. For academic counselling and personal support, visit the Guidance Office or sign up through the Student Portal.

How to Join

  • Check the Student Portal for club sign-up forms and meeting schedules.
  • Attend club fairs at the start of each term to meet club leaders.
  • Contact Student Support or your homeroom teacher for mentorship enrollment.
Want to add a club or event? Teachers and student leaders can email the Activities Coordinator to request a listing.
